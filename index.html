BACK TO THE GAME
This game idea came as an experiment for a publishing house's own website, Lagosta Press — and ended up too fun to leave there, so we gave it a home of its own to make it accessible to more people. The plan is to keep building it and making it better, so please send us your suggestions. It's a work by Eduardo Menezes with a huge collaboration from Joel Souza, as well as a handful of friends who tested the mechanics. Thanks to all of them.
Lagosta is lobster in Portuguese, by the way.
